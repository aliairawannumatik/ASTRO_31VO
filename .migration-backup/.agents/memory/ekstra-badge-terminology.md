---
name: EKSTRA difficulty badge terminology
description: Translation for the EKSTRA (bonus/composite problem) difficulty badge, distinct from MUDAH/SEDANG/SULIT tiers.
---

Some pages (e.g. Kelas 9 Transformasi Geometri Dilatasi) have a 4th difficulty badge beyond MUDAH/SEDANG/SULIT: **EKSTRA**, used for composite/bonus problems (not necessarily "hardest").

Translation decided: EKSTRA → **Extra** (EN) / **発展問題** (JA).

**Why:** "Extra" better reflects it being a bonus/composite exercise rather than a difficulty escalation beyond SULIT ("Hard"); avoids confusion with a "harder than hard" tier.

**How to apply:** When adding trilingual badges to a page with an EKSTRA-tier problem, reuse this exact mapping for consistency (companion to MUDAH=Easy/基本, SEDANG=Medium/標準, SULIT=Hard/発展 in terminology-rules.md).
